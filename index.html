Planned Curve Integrity — Two Confirmations

QuestionAnswerFinding
Does the planned S-curve exclude the 70-day non-billable Owner activity?NO — and it just grewThe activity is C2600 “Site: Live Nation Specialties — by Owner,” 70 days, 11-Mar-27 → 17-Jun-27. The 8/25/26 export adds a second Owner-furnished line, Cx1060 “Site: AV & IT Install — by Owner,” 20 days, 27-May-27 → 24-Jun-27, which was absent from both the 7/28 and 8/4 exports. Owner-furnished scope in the schedule has grown from 70 to 90 days across two activities. The planned % curve is 23 hand-entered values, never derived from the P6 export — and could not have been, because the export is duration-only (no cost loading, no budgeted value, no activity % complete). Nothing in its construction removes C2600.
Does that distort the current −14.66% variance?No effect yetC2600 does not start until 11-Mar-27, seven months after the 7/31/2026 period end (P6 data date 4-Aug-26). None of its progress sits inside the 59% planned figure for July. The July variance is clean.
Is CCI responsible for those 90 days?No — Owner scopeBoth activities are Owner-performed and Owner-furnished. CCI neither performs nor bills them, and should not be held to schedule progress on them. They are also not on the critical path to TCO: they run parallel to CCI closeout, finishing 17-Jun-27 (C2600) and 24-Jun-27 (Cx1060), while TCO on 09-Jul-27 is driven by CCI’s own Cx1010 Final Testing / Cx / TaB (30 days, 27-May-27 → 09-Jul-27) together with Owner’s Punch Corrections and Music Hall drapery.
So what is the real risk?Interface, not delayCx1060 (AV & IT Install) starts 27-May-27, the same day CCI’s commissioning begins. If commissioning, testing or balancing depends on Owner AV/IT being energised and in place, an Owner slip flows straight into CCI’s critical path — and CCI carries roughly $306,000/month of General Conditions while it waits. Confirm the logic ties between Cx1060 and Cx1010 before accepting that risk allocation.
Where does it bite the curve?The tailThe planned curve climbs 96.5% (Mar-27) → 99.7% (Jun-27) — exactly the window both C2600 and Cx1060 occupy. Because that segment carries Owner scope CCI is not responsible for, CCI’s billings can never reach the planned 100% even with flawless performance. The schedule’s finish line and CCI’s finish line are not the same line, and the planned curve should be read as an Owner-side view of the whole project rather than a CCI performance baseline in its final months.
Is there a basis mismatch?YesActual % divides by CCI’s contract sum ($45,492,220), which excludes Owner-furnished specialties. Planned % reflects whole-schedule progress including C2600. The two curves do not share a denominator in the final four months.
Is planned retention release built into the S-curve?NO — correctly absentNeither curve contains a retention concept. Planned % is pure schedule progress with no cash element. Actual % is Completed & Stored ÷ Contract Sum — retainage-independent (re-running PA#11 with retainage still withheld returns the identical 44.34%). The curve terminates at 100% = all work in place, a production event, not a payment event.
Is there any retention left to release?None remainingRetainage went $871,605.49 → $0.00 at PA#11 — fully released. There is no retention balance to model at substantial completion. CCI’s billing projection likewise carries no retention row (its lines are Div 1–33, Allowances and Change Orders only); its end-gap — now $1,896,850 — is unbilled change order value and forecast underrun, not retention.
What cannot be determined from the available data: the dollar value of the Live Nation Specialties scope, and therefore the exact size of the tail distortion. The P6 export carries no cost loading, so the planned curve’s weighting of C2600 is unknown — it may have been implicitly excluded when the curve was drawn, or implicitly included. Resolving this requires either a cost-loaded P6 export from CCI or the contract value of the Owner-furnished specialties package. Until then, treat the planned curve’s final four months as approximate. Neither Owner activity has started as of the 8/25/26 data date, so nothing in the −19.16% variance to date is attributable to scope CCI does not control — that variance stands on CCI-performed work alone.
